## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an industrial electro-mechanical workshop and service hub. It marries the dependable, systematic architecture of modern structured portals (reminiscent of enterprise internal sites and modular workspaces) with an authoritative, industrial engineering tone. 

The aesthetic is clean, functional, and organized:
- **Visual Personality:** Reliable, mechanical, certified, precision-engineered, and uncluttered.
- **Target Audience:** Industrial plant managers, procurement engineers, equipment technicians, and business clients requiring electric motor maintenance, rewinding, and diagnostic services.
- **Design Movement:** Modern Corporate/Industrial Minimalism. It features alternating full-width contextual section containers, distinct card-based module arrangements, precise border delineations, and clear hierarchy reminiscent of systematic site builders.
- **Atmosphere:** Solid and calm like an immaculate machine floor, accented with crisp electric energy.

## Colors

The color system delivers high legibility and operational clarity:

- **Primary (`#0A2540` - Deep Industrial Navy):** Represents stability, heavy electrical engineering, and structural foundation. Used for key headlines, dominant hero banners, navigation bars, primary action triggers, and primary brand markers.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7` - Electric Cyan / Motor Blue):** Represents energized currents, precision testing, and diagnostic instruments. Used for active states, link interactions, progress indicators, focus rings, and primary data highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` - Workshop Amber / Safety Gold):** Used sparingly as an industrial alert and verification cue—status badges (such as "In Inspection" or "Urgent Rewind"), warning highlights, and certification tags.
- **Neutral (`#475569` - Slate Carbon):** Calibrated for body copy, subheaders, structural hairline dividers, and secondary component iconography.
- **Background Rhythm:** Surfaces alternate systematically between clean white (`#FFFFFF`) and architectural cool slate (`#F8FAFC`), framing sections cleanly without visual noise.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on mechanical precision and high-efficiency scanning:

- **Headlines (`Roboto Flex`):** Provides a robust, mechanical presence with geometric square curves and exact letterforms. It anchors banners, section title bars, and table headers.
- **Body Text (`Open Sans`):** Selected for its humanist openness and outstanding readability in technical documentation, service checklists, and specifications.
- **Labels & Data Tags (`Roboto Flex`):** Set with a slightly tracked uppercase or semi-bold weight to mirror technical nameplates, rating labels, and electrical spec-sheets.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model takes inspiration from clean, modular section-based page builders:

- **Full-Width Canvas with Centered Content Grid:** Alternating horizontal section blocks stretch edge-to-edge with alternating backgrounds (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`). Internal content conforms to a centered 12-column grid capped at 1200px maximum width.
- **Banner Headers:** Top-level headers span 100% width with a fixed-ratio or constrained height (280px–360px on desktop, 180px–220px on mobile), utilizing primary navy tone overlays, high-contrast title typography, and breadcrumb navigation.
- **Modular Blocks:** Components live within explicit, self-contained sections separated by crisp hairline boundaries (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- **Responsive Behavior:** 
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 2rem`. Section vertical padding set to `space-xl` (2.5rem to 4rem).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, components drop from 3-4 columns to 2 columns.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout, gutters tighten to `1rem`, sections stack linearly, and card grids collapse into single vertical sequences.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids excessive drop shadows, prioritizing structural separation, tonal variation, and subtle borders:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Base Canvas:** Alternating `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`.
  - **Elevated Cards:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`).
  - **Active / Interactive Hover:** Subtle ambient rise created with `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.08)` and border emphasis shifting to `#0284C7`.
- **Top Banner & Navigation:** The site navigation rests flat at standard state with a bottom hairline divider (`#E2E8F0`); upon scroll, it adheres with a faint ambient shadow `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.06)`.
- **Low-Contrast Dividers:** Crisp mechanical separator lines (`#E2E8F0`) are used consistently across table rows, list items, and section closures to maintain an organized filing structure.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses the `Soft` (Level 1) system to convey engineered discipline:

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Form fields, input elements, inline code/spec badges, and technical chips.
- **Medium / Card Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Workshop service cards, modal dialogs, diagnostic report containers, and media tiles.
- **Large Radius (0.75rem / 12px):** Hero feature banners and callout highlight boxes.
- **Buttons & Action Triggers:** Standardized at 4px corner radius to evoke industrial equipment pushbuttons and professional enterprise tools.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#0A2540` background, pure white text, 4px border radius, medium label weight. Hover shifts to `#0284C7` with a 150ms ease transition.
- **Secondary:** Outlined with a 1.5px border in `#0A2540`, transparent background, `#0A2540` text. Hover fills with `#F1F5F9`.
- **Subtle / Ghost:** Transparent background with `#0284C7` text, used within technical data tables and inline actions.

### Cards & Workshop Modules
- Structured white containers (`#FFFFFF`) with 8px radius, framed by a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`).
- Headers within cards feature an integrated gray accent bar (`#F8FAFC`) with bottom border divider, cleanly separating metadata from card body details.
- Used for service offerings (e.g., "Dynamic Balancing", "Stator Rewinding", "Vibration Analysis").

### Chips & Technical Badges
- Compact rectangular badges (4px radius) with uppercase label typography.
- **Status Types:**
  - *Certified / Active:* Pale cyan background (`#E0F2FE`), cyan text (`#0284C7`).
  - *Testing / Amber:* Warm amber background (`#FEF3C7`), dark amber text (`#D97706`).
  - *Specification / Neutral:* Cool gray background (`#F1F5F9`), slate text (`#475569`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid border `#CBD5E1`, 4px corner radius.
- Focus state reveals a 2px outer outline in electric blue (`#0284C7`) with 0px offset.
- Floating or distinct top-aligned labels rendered in `Roboto Flex` label-md.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 4px radius on checkboxes, full circular radius on radio buttons.
- Inactive state uses 1.5px border `#94A3B8`.
- Checked state fills with `#0A2540` and displays a crisp white checkmark or inner dot.

### Lists & Technical Spec Sheets
- Key-value pairing format: left-aligned slate label (`#475569`), right-aligned bold navy data value (`#0A2540`).
- Separated by hairline dividers (`#E2E8F0`) with `space-sm` vertical padding.

### Section Dividers & Banner Strips
- Prominent banner header container at the page crown: `#0A2540` solid or subtle engineering grid overlay, featuring high-contrast white header text and service status highlights.
- Clear structural transition dividers between sections using 1px `#E2E8F0` rules or alternating solid `#F8FAFC` full-bleed containers.
